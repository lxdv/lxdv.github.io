I started out in ML back in 2017, then got hooked on computer vision in the OpenCV world: consulting projects, a 3D scene reconstruction system that made it to Apple WWDC 2020, and a patent along the way. At [Modelplace.AI](https://modelplace.ai) I tried myself as a tech lead. Then I co-founded KopiKat and found out the hard way what it's like to do everything yourself, from writing the code to closing the deals.

These days I build agentic systems at [Sumsub](https://sumsub.com) and vibe-code pretty much everything else.

## Work experience

- **2025 – present** · AI Things @ [Sumsub](https://sumsub.com)

  Agentic systems for a global identity verification and fraud prevention platform.

- **2023 – 2024** · Co-founder & CTO @ KopiKat

  Came up with the core tech: generative augmentation that helps computer vision models cope with hard cases. Owned everything technical and sold the product to enterprise clients.

- **2022 – 2025** · Computer Vision @ [OpenCV.AI](https://opencv.ai)

  Led teams building computer vision solutions for clients and for the company's own products.

- **2020 – 2022** · Technical Lead @ [Modelplace.AI](https://modelplace.ai)

  Set the direction and architecture for an AI model marketplace, from scratch to launch.

- **2018 – 2020** · Deep Learning Engineer @ [Xperience.AI](https://xperience.ai)

  Computer vision consulting. The 3D scene reconstruction project was shown at Apple WWDC 2020 and led to a [US patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/US12026840B2) where I'm a co-inventor.

- **2017 – 2018** · Data Scientist @ [Neuron.digital](https://neuron.digital)

  NLP models for sentiment analysis, document classification and entity extraction.

## Education

- **2018 – 2020** · Master in Data Mining @ Higher School of Economics
- **2014 – 2018** · Bachelor in Applied Mathematics and Information Science @ Higher School of Economics
